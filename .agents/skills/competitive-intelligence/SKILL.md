# SKILL: Adversarial Competitive Intelligence & Moat Analysis

> **Origin:** Original NEXUS Enterprise Skill (Proprietary Workforce Asset)  
> **Classification:** Strategic Market Intelligence Standard  
> **Author:** NEXUS Research & Intelligence Board  
> **Target Owner:** NAYA (Director of Research & Intelligence)

---

## 1. OBJECTIVE
To deliver forensic, real-time, and actionable intelligence on direct and indirect competitors, dissecting their business models, product features, pricing strategies, customer vulnerabilities, and economic moats (Hamilton Helmer's 7 Powers) to guide NEXUS positioning.

## 2. SCOPE
- Competitive teardowns, pricing structure reverse-engineering, patent/tech stack inspection, customer sentiment and churn analysis, and sales battle card synthesis.

## 3. EXECUTION METHODOLOGY
- **Hamilton Helmer's 7 Powers Moat Assessment:** Scale Economies, Network Effects, Counter-Positioning, Switching Costs, Branding, Cornered Resource, Process Power.
- **Competitor Reverse-Engineering:** Analyzing public SEC/financial filings, hiring trends (job postings revealing tech bets), changelogs, customer review sentiment (G2, Capterra, Gartner Peer Insights).
- **Adversarial War-Gaming:** Simulating how key competitors will counter-attack when NEXUS enters their market.

## 4. DECISION RULES & CRITERIA
1. Never assess a competitor as "having no weaknesses." Every competitor has structural trade-offs (e.g., enterprise legacy systems move slowly; nimble startups lack compliance).
2. Classify competitor features into: Table Stakes (mandatory parity), Delighters (differentiators), and Bloatware (unwanted complexity).
3. Identify the "Achilles' Heel" for each tier-1 competitor (e.g., vendor lock-in, opaque pricing, poor customer support).

## 5. QUALITY STANDARDS
- All pricing models must cite date of observation and packaging tiers.
- Customer sentiment claims must be backed by a minimum of 20 verified enterprise reviews.
- Sales battle cards must provide exact responses to common competitive objections.

## 6. CONSTRAINTS
- Strict adherence to ethical intelligence gathering: Do not use illegal corporate espionage, pretexting, or breach non-disclosure agreements.
- Do not rely on competitor marketing claims at face value; cross-reference with real customer deployments.

## 7. EXPECTED OUTPUT FORMAT
- **Competitor Profiling Matrix:** Tier 1 and Tier 2 player overview.
- **7 Powers Moat Evaluation Table:** Score (1-5) per power for each rival.
- **Pricing & Packaging Reverse-Engineering:** Tier comparisons and hidden fees.
- **Competitor Battle Card:** Strengths, weaknesses, "How to Win", and landmine questions for sales reps.

## 8. EXAMPLE USAGE
NAYA produces a sales battle card against a dominant enterprise SaaS incumbent, highlighting their 18-month deployment lag and 300% price hikes at contract renewal.
