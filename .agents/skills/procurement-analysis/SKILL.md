# SKILL: Enterprise Procurement & Vendor Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) Analysis

> **Origin:** Original NEXUS Enterprise Skill (Proprietary Workforce Asset)  
> **Classification:** Corporate Fiscal Governance Standard  
> **Author:** NEXUS Finance & Compliance Board  
> **Target Owner:** SEKAR (Director of Finance, Risk & Compliance)

---

## 1. OBJECTIVE
To maximize enterprise capital efficiency, eliminate wasteful software license bloat, conduct objective Request for Proposal (RFP) evaluations, calculate 3-year Total Cost of Ownership (TCO), and prevent predatory vendor lock-in.

## 2. SCOPE
- Vendor RFP scoring, Total Cost of Ownership calculations (license fees, implementation, training, maintenance, data egress), vendor risk assessments, contract SLA negotiation parameters, and software asset management.

## 3. EXECUTION METHODOLOGY
- **Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) Decomposition:**
  $$\text{TCO} = \text{Acquisition Cost} + \text{Implementation / Migration} + \text{Operational Maintenance} + \text{Cloud Egress \& Overages} - \text{Salvage / Residual Value}$$
- **Weighted RFP Vendor Scoring Matrix:** Technical Fit (35%), Financial / TCO (30%), Security & Compliance (20%), Vendor Viability & Support SLA (15%).
- **Vendor Lock-in Evaluation:** Assessing data portability, open standards compatibility, and switching cost penalties.

## 4. DECISION RULES & AUDIT CRITERIA
1. Mandatory 3-Bid Rule: Any procurement commitment exceeding $10,000 / Rp 150 Juta annually mandates evaluation of at least three competing vendors or an internal build option.
2. Auto-Renewal Prohibition: All vendor contracts must require explicit 60-day written notice for renewal and prohibit unilateral price escalation clauses exceeding CPI inflation.
3. Vendors handling corporate or customer data must possess verified SOC 2 Type II or ISO 27001 certifications.

## 5. QUALITY STANDARDS
- TCO must be calculated across a minimum horizon of 36 months to account for renewal price hikes.
- Scoring rationales must be objective and documented in the audit ledger.

## 6. CONSTRAINTS
- Never accept vendor list prices as final; standard enterprise discounts (20%-45%) must be factored into negotiation targets.
- Do not approve procurement for software duplicating capabilities already active in the corporate stack.

## 7. EXPECTED OUTPUT FORMAT
- **Procurement Evaluation Memorandum**
- **3-Year TCO Comparison Table (Vendor A vs. Vendor B vs. In-House)**
- **Weighted Vendor Scoring Matrix**
- **Contract Negotiation Leverage Points & Redlines**
- **Procurement Sign-off Recommendation**

## 8. EXAMPLE USAGE
SEKAR evaluates competing cloud database providers, discovering that Vendor A has cheaper compute but 400% higher data transfer egress fees, saving the company Rp 320 Juta over 3 years by selecting Vendor B.
