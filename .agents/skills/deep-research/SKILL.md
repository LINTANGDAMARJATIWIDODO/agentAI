## 1. PURPOSE & OBJECTIVE
To conduct exhaustive, multi-hop deep investigations into complex markets, emerging technologies, macroeconomic conditions, and regulatory environments, synthesizing primary and secondary sources into peer-reviewed research deliverables.

## 2. SCOPE
- Query variant generation, academic paper synthesis (arXiv, IEEE, ScienceDirect), industry quarterly reports (IDC, Gartner, Statista), government regulatory filings, and multi-source cross-verification.

## 3. MULTI-HOP RESEARCH METHODOLOGY
```
[User Problem / Topic]
        |
        v
[1. Query Expansion (3-5 Keyword Variants)]
        |
        v
[2. Parallel Source Harvest (Web / Docs / Financials)]
        |
        v
[3. Triangulation & Source Credibility Scoring]
        |
        v
[4. Synthesis: Facts vs. Assumptions vs. Strategic Insights]
        |
        v
[5. Formal Bibliography & Provenance Indexing]
```

## 4. STEP-BY-STEP PROCEDURE
1. **Deconstruct Topic:** Identify core questions, required empirical metrics, and geographic/temporal boundaries.
2. **Execute Targeted Queries:** Formulate specific search strings combining industry entities, years, and metrics.
3. **Triangulate Findings:** Require at least two independent credible sources before classifying a number or trend as a `[VERIFIED FACT]`.
4. **Isolate Gaps & Biases:** Identify commercial sponsor bias in industry reports and note sample size limitations.
5. **Compile Research Dossier:** Structure report with executive abstract, data tables, analytical frameworks, and formal citations.

## 5. CITATION STANDARD & METADATA FORMAT
Every data point must include:
- Organization / Author Name.
- Title of Publication.
- Publication Date / Year.
- Direct Reference Link or Identifier.

## 6. CONSTRAINTS
- Prohibit citing unverified personal forum posts or promotional SEO affiliate blogs as authoritative enterprise data.
- Explicitly flag any estimate where standard deviation across sources exceeds 25%.
