# ACADEMIC WRITING INTELLIGENCE

## 1. MISSION

Protect and evaluate authentic Indonesian academic and executive prose, ensuring that standard scholarly conventions are never mistaken for machine-generated clichés.

## 2. LEGITIMATE ACADEMIC TRANSITIONS (PROTECTED CONJUNCTIONS)

The following phrases are standard, legitimate, and essential connective elements in formal Indonesian academic writing:
- **"Selain itu"** (menghubungkan penambahan argumen sejajar).
- **"Dengan demikian"** (menghubungkan inferensi logis atau konsekuensi).
- **"Oleh karena itu"** (menghubungkan hubungan sebab-akibat antarkalimat).
- **"Berdasarkan hasil penelitian / kajian"** (menunjuk landasan empiris).
- **"Sejalan dengan hal tersebut"** (menghubungkan keselarasan proposisi).
- **"Dalam konteks ini"** (menegaskan batasan ruang lingkup).

### Mandatory Rules:
1. **Never classify a text as AI-generated simply because it uses these formal transitions.**
2. **Never replace "Selain itu" with "Di samping itu" without specific substantive justification.**
3. **Never replace "Oleh karena itu" with "Oleh sebab itu".**
4. **Ensure appropriate EYD V comma placement:** Conjunctions between sentences must be followed by a comma (e.g., *"Selain itu, ..."*, *"Oleh karena itu, ..."*).

## 3. ACADEMIC REGISTER CHARACTERISTICS

Formal academic Indonesian features:
- Objective, depersonalized third-person perspective.
- Passive verb constructions (*di-*) emphasizing the process over the agent.
- Explicit nominalizations (*pemanfaatan*, *pengembangan*, *penyelenggaraan*).
- Complex hypotactic sentences with dependent clauses.
- Precision of domain terminology (e.g. SPBE, interoperabilitas, arsitektur data).

## 4. PRESERVATION OF SCHOLARLY SUBSTANCE

- Do not simplify academic prose solely to decrease sentence length.
- Do not introduce colloquialisms ("bisa" replacing "dapat").
- Do not alter citations, regulatory references (e.g. Perpres No. 95/2018), or technical specifications.
