# SKILL: Executive Decision Intelligence & Strategic Trade-off Analysis

> **Origin:** Original NEXUS Enterprise Skill (Proprietary Workforce Asset)  
> **Classification:** Executive Leadership Standard  
> **Author:** NEXUS Architecture & Governance Board  
> **Target Owner:** ARKA (Chief Executive & Strategy Officer)

---

## 1. OBJECTIVE
To provide a structured, mathematically sound, and cognitive-bias-free methodology for making high-stakes corporate decisions by quantifying trade-offs, evaluating multi-criteria options, and formulating clear strategic verdicts for the Board of Directors.

## 2. SCOPE
- Capital allocation trade-offs, technology build-vs-buy evaluations, market entry timing decisions, organizational restructuring, and risk-reward optimization.

## 3. EXECUTION METHODOLOGY
- **Multi-Criteria Decision Analysis (MCDA):** Weighting strategic priorities (e.g., Time to Market 30%, Cost 25%, Scalability 25%, Regulatory Risk 20%) and scoring competing options.
- **Pre-Mortem Analysis:** Projecting 12 months into the future, assuming the chosen option has failed catastrophically, and tracing back the vulnerabilities to remediate them proactively.
- **Reversibility Categorization (Type 1 vs. Type 2 Decisions):**
  - *Type 1 (One-Way Door):* Irreversible or highly consequential decisions requiring exhaustive validation and human board approval.
  - *Type 2 (Two-Way Door):* Reversible decisions that should be executed rapidly with delegated authority.

## 4. DECISION RULES & CRITERIA
1. If an option carries an unmitigated Critical Regulatory or Legal Risk, it is disqualified immediately regardless of ROI.
2. If two options differ by less than 5% on weighted score, choose the one with higher reversibility and lower upfront capital expenditure (CAPEX).
3. Every strategic recommendation must explicitly detail the "Cost of Inaction" (what the company loses if nothing is done).

## 5. QUALITY STANDARDS & ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
- Decision matrix weights must total exactly 100%.
- Scoring rationales for each criterion must be accompanied by empirical justification from divisional findings.
- The pre-mortem risk mitigations must be concrete and actionable.

## 6. CONSTRAINTS
- DURATION BIAS: Do not reject an option solely because it requires more upfront planning if its long-term NPV is substantially superior.
- Sunk cost fallacy: Never justify continuing a failing initiative based on capital already spent.

## 7. EXPECTED OUTPUT FORMAT
- **Executive Strategic Verdict Memo:** Summary of decision context.
- **Weighted Multi-Criteria Decision Matrix Table:** Options vs. Weighted Criteria.
- **Pre-Mortem Vulnerability Analysis:** Identified failure modes and early countermeasures.
- **Actionable Execution Directive:** Next steps, owners, and review checkpoints.

## 8. EXAMPLE USAGE
ARKA evaluates whether to build a proprietary on-premise LLM cluster (Option A) vs. utilize enterprise-isolated cloud APIs (Option B) for a banking client, weighing data sovereignty, CAPEX, latency, and operational overhead.
