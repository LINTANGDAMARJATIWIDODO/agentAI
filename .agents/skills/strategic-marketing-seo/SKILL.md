# SKILL: Enterprise Strategic Marketing & Technical B2B SEO

> **Origin:** Original NEXUS Enterprise Skill (Proprietary Workforce Asset)  
> **Classification:** Growth & Commercial Strategy Standard  
> **Author:** NEXUS Operations & Growth Board  
> **Target Owner:** DIMAS (Director of Operations & Business Growth)

---

## 1. OBJECTIVE
To architect inbound customer acquisition flywheels, build organic enterprise search dominance through technical SEO, capture high-intent commercial keywords, and convert enterprise decision-makers through educational thought leadership.

## 2. SCOPE
- Technical SEO (Core Web Vitals, Structured Data / JSON-LD, crawl efficiency), high-intent keyword clustering, B2B demand generation funnels, account-based marketing (ABM), and content hub architecture.

## 3. EXECUTION METHODOLOGY
- **Search Intent Taxonomy:** Informational -> Navigational -> Commercial Investigation -> Transactional. Prioritize Commercial Investigation and Transactional keywords with high buyer intent.
- **Topical Authority Hub-and-Spoke Model:** Comprehensive pillar pages (e.g. "Complete Guide to Enterprise Multi-Agent Governance") supported by deep cluster articles covering specific sub-topics.
- **B2B Demand Engine:** Paid search capture for high-intent keywords paired with long-form whitepapers, ROI calculators, and interactive demos.

## 4. DECISION RULES & SEO CRITERIA
1. Quality Over Fluff: B2B buyers reject generic AI-generated thin content. Articles must provide proprietary diagrams, verifiable data, and actionable implementation advice.
2. Structured Data: Every technical guide and case study must feature valid Schema.org JSON-LD markup (`Article`, `SoftwareApplication`, `FAQPage`).
3. Alignment with SEKAR's CAC Target: Paid acquisition channels are disqualified if Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) yields an LTV:CAC ratio < 3.0x.

## 5. QUALITY STANDARDS & ACCREDITATION
- Mobile-friendly, high-contrast typography, fast loading times (< 1.5s LCP).
- Clear, non-intrusive Call to Action (CTA) linking to enterprise consultation or live product demo.

## 6. CONSTRAINTS
- Strict prohibition of black-hat SEO tactics (keyword stuffing, private blog networks / PBNs, cloaking).
- Do not make misleading performance claims in marketing copy that violate trade regulations.

## 7. EXPECTED OUTPUT FORMAT
- **Strategic Marketing Plan & Funnel Architecture**
- **Keyword Research & Search Intent Clustering Table**
- **Content Pillar & Hub-and-Spoke Editorial Roadmap**
- **Technical SEO Audit & On-Page Optimization Checklist**
- **Demand Generation KPI Projections (Traffic, MQLs, SQLs, Projected CAC)**

## 8. EXAMPLE USAGE
DIMAS designs an organic search and content marketing strategy targeting keywords like "enterprise multi-agent orchestration framework" and "AI agent security compliance," projecting 12,000 monthly high-intent B2B visits within 6 months.
