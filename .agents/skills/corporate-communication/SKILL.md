# SKILL: Enterprise Corporate Communications & Crisis PR Governance

> **Origin:** Original NEXUS Enterprise Skill (Proprietary Workforce Asset)  
> **Classification:** Executive PR & Stakeholder Relations Standard  
> **Author:** NEXUS Operations & Growth Board  
> **Target Owner:** DIMAS (Director of Operations) | Shared: ARKA (CEO)

---

## 1. OBJECTIVE
To protect and enhance corporate reputation, articulate executive strategy with precision, manage investor relations, execute proactive crisis communication, and align internal teams during organizational transformations.

## 2. SCOPE
- Press releases, quarterly shareholder letters, board update presentations, crisis communications (data breaches, outages, executive changes), and internal company town hall messaging.

## 3. CRISIS COMMUNICATION PROTOCOL (THE 5C FRAMEWORK)
1. **Care:** Immediate acknowledgment of the issue and expression of concern for affected clients/users.
2. **Commitment:** Stating the exact executive commitment to resolving the situation with dedicated resources.
3. **Context:** Providing factual, transparent explanation of what occurred without shifting blame.
4. **Correction:** Documenting specific, immediate steps taken to fix the immediate problem.
5. **Consistency:** Maintaining transparent, scheduled updates across all official communication channels.

## 4. DECISION RULES & EDITORIAL CRITERIA
1. No Speculation: During a crisis or major corporate event, communications must state only confirmed facts. Never speculate on causes before technical forensics from RAKA are complete.
2. The 1-Hour First Response Rule: In the event of a critical security or operational incident, a holding statement must be issued within 60 minutes acknowledging receipt and ongoing investigation.
3. Every public release must have formal sign-off from both DIMAS (PR/Comms) and ARKA (Executive Authority).

## 5. QUALITY STANDARDS & TONE
- Professional, empathetic, transparent, and legally defensible.
- Free of jargon, bureaucratic obfuscation, and defensive rhetoric.

## 6. CONSTRAINTS
- Never release unverified statements during active legal disputes or regulatory investigations without human legal counsel clearance.
- Prohibit misleading or selective quotation of data in investor or public communications.

## 7. EXPECTED OUTPUT FORMAT
- **Executive Communications Strategy Memo**
- **Draft Press Release / Stakeholder Briefing (Headline, Dateline, Body, Boilerplate)**
- **Crisis Response Holding Statements & FAQ Response Grid**
- **Internal Employee Alignment Script**
- **Distribution Channels & Media Outreach Plan**

## 8. EXAMPLE USAGE
DIMAS crafts an executive press release announcing NEXUS AI Enterprise's partnership with leading regional banks, followed by a Q&A talking points memo for C-suite media interviews.
