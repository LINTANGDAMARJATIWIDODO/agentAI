### 1. FINANCIAL MODELING STANDARDS
All financial feasibility analyses and budget proposals produced within NEXUS AI ENTERPRISE must adhere to the following standards:

1. **Dual Currency Representation:** All models must present figures in IDR (Indonesian Rupiah) and USD (United States Dollar) with an explicitly declared exchange rate baseline (e.g. 1 USD = Rp 16.000).
2. **Horizon Projections:** Standard investment evaluations require a 36-month (3-year) pro-forma monthly and quarterly forecast.
3. **Discount Rate (WACC):** The standard corporate Weighted Average Cost of Capital used for Net Present Value (NPV) discounting is 12.0% per annum, unless specific risk premiums apply.

### 2. CAPEX vs. OPEX ALLOCATION RULES
- **CAPEX (Capital Expenditure):** Hardware acquisition, perpetual software licenses, initial custom development capitalization, legal entity incorporation fees. Depreciated over 3 to 5 years straight-line.
- **OPEX (Operating Expenditure):** Cloud infrastructure hosting, AI model API inference tokens, ongoing software subscriptions, salaries/contractors, performance marketing, legal retainer.

### 3. INVESTMENT ACCEPTANCE BENCHMARKS
A strategic initiative is considered financially viable if it meets the following hurdle rates:
- **Payback Period (BEP):** <= 24 months.
- **Net Present Value (NPV):** > 0 at a 12% discount rate.
- **Internal Rate of Return (IRR):** >= 20% annualized.
- **Unit Economics Health:** Lifetime Value to Customer Acquisition Cost (LTV : CAC) ratio >= 3.0x.
- **Gross Margin Target:** >= 65% for software/technology initiatives.
